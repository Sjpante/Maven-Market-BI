## 💡 Key Business Insights & Highlights

### 1. Sales Overview
* **High-Level Metrics:** Tracked Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Transactions, and overall Return Rate.
* **Geographic Reach:** Mapped store locations across 3 countries (USA, Mexico, and Canada).
* **Monthly Tracking:** Monitored current month KPIs against explicit target goals.
* **Forecasting:** Built a Revenue Trend & Forecasting line visual to project growth trajectories.

### 2. Product & Profitability Analysis
* **What-If Price Sensitivity:** Implemented a dynamic slider parameter to simulate scenario modeling on price adjustments.
* **Target Gauges:** Visualized real-time performance against monthly targets.
* **Multi-Level Charting:** Built comprehensive multi-metric charts to analyze performance across product categories.

### 3. Customer Demographics
* **Customer Base Insights:** Highlighted key customer counts and average revenue metrics using dedicated KPI cards.
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Categorized transaction volumes by customer income levels and occupations.
* **Top Customer Tracking:** Identified and ranked top individual accounts by revenue and purchase volume.
